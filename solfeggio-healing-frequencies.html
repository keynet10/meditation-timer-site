Understanding Solfeggio

A world of frequency and sound.

Solfeggio frequencies are a collection of tones traditionally associated with meditation, spiritual practice, sound healing and personal wellbeing. Different frequencies have developed their own traditions and associations, with 528 Hz perhaps being the best known. Today, many people listen to these frequencies while meditating, practising mindfulness, relaxing, studying, reading or preparing for sleep. The experience is highly personal. Rather than thinking of a frequency as a medical treatment, it can be useful to think of frequency listening as another tool for creating a calm, intentional environment.

The Famous 528 Hz Frequency

Why is 528 Hz so popular?

528 Hz is probably the most widely recognised of the modern Solfeggio frequencies. It is often referred to as the “Love Frequency” and has become particularly popular in meditation, relaxation and sound-healing communities.

Many listeners simply enjoy its tone and use it as a calming background during meditation, yoga, relaxation or quiet reflection. Its popularity makes 528 Hz an interesting frequency to explore, while individual experiences naturally vary.

Evidence & Context

What does the science say?

It is important to distinguish between the broader benefits of music, relaxation and meditation and claims specifically attributed to individual Solfeggio frequencies.

Research has found that music-based interventions can have beneficial effects on psychological and physiological measures of stress, while research into meditation has also found benefits for areas such as anxiety and psychological wellbeing.

However, strong clinical evidence that a particular Solfeggio frequency can directly heal a disease or produce a specific medical outcome is currently limited. Solfeggio listening is therefore best viewed as a complementary wellness practice, not a medical treatment.

Frequency listening is an individual experience. Choose sounds that you enjoy and that help create the environment you want for meditation, relaxation, sleep or focus.

What are Solfeggio frequencies?
