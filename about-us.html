The Story

It Started With a Simple Idea

I was looking for a simple meditation timer with interval bells. I didn't want a massive app filled with guided meditations, streaks, social engagement or features I didn't need. I simply wanted to set a meditation time, have a bell at intervals and get on with my practice.

I couldn't find the app I was looking for, so I decided to create it myself. That became Meditation Bell Timer.

“I wasn't looking for a complicated wellness platform. I just wanted a simple timer that did what I needed.”

What started as a personal solution worked so well that I began thinking about other ways simple sound-based tools could be used for meditation, relaxation and mindful listening.

That led me to explore Solfeggio frequencies. I found the ideas surrounding frequencies fascinating and decided to create Solfeggio Healing Frequencies, giving people a straightforward way to explore and listen to a range of frequencies.

Then the two ideas came together. I wanted to combine frequencies with the calming atmosphere of ambient nature sounds, allowing people to create their own sound environment. That became Soundbath Zen.

Our Philosophy

Meditation Doesn't Have to Be Complicated

The philosophy behind Future Home Tek is straightforward: create useful tools that stay out of the way.

01 — SIMPLE

Keep It Simple

We focus on the features that are genuinely useful. A timer should be easy to set. A sound should be easy to play. The experience should feel calm rather than complicated.

02 — YOUR PRACTICE

You Stay in Control

There is no single right way to meditate. Our apps are designed to give you tools and choices while leaving the practice itself in your hands.

03 — LESS DISTRACTION

Quiet by Design

We believe meditation and mindful listening work best when the technology becomes part of the background rather than the centre of attention.
