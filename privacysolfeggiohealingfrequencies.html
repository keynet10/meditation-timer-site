Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Solfeggio: Healing Frequencies mobile application (the "App").

We believe that your listening experience is entirely your own business. We have built this project with a strict privacy-first approach.

1. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • No Accounts: No sign-up is required for the App.
  • No Tracking: We do not track individual usage, session lengths, or your specific audio preferences.
  • Local Storage: Your settings are saved locally on your own device and are never sent to a server.

2. Advertising

Solfeggio: Healing Frequencies is a premium, user-supported project. We do not host advertisements, and we do not sell your "attention" or data to third parties.

3. Data Deletion

Because we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers, we do not hold any personal user data to delete. All app settings and preferences are stored exclusively locally on your device. You can delete this local data at any time by clearing the App's data or cache in your device settings, or by completely uninstalling the App.

4. Changes to This Policy

Any future updates will only be to reflect changes in legal requirements or new features. Our core philosophy of zero data collection will not change.
