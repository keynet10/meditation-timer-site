Frequently Asked Questions

The simple version.

Are the apps subscription based?

No. The apps are designed around a simple one-time purchase model for premium features rather than recurring monthly subscriptions.

Do the apps require an account?

No account is required to use the apps. They are designed to be straightforward, private and easy to use.

Do the apps work offline?

Yes. The apps are designed to work offline, allowing you to use your sessions without relying on an internet connection.

Which app should I choose?

Choose Meditation Bell Timer if you want a traditional timer with bells and ambient sounds. Choose Solfeggio if you want a focused frequency library. Choose Sound Bath Zen if you want to combine frequencies and ambient sounds into your own customised sessions.

Can I use the apps for sleep and relaxation?

Yes. All three apps can be used as part of personal relaxation or bedtime routines. Sound selection, frequency choice and session duration can be tailored to your own preferences.
