Frequently Asked Questions

Our Apps

Are the apps suitable for beginners?

Yes. All three apps are designed to be simple to understand. You can open an app, choose your preferred sound or frequency, set a session and begin without needing to complete a course or follow complicated instructions.

Can I use the apps without an internet connection?

Yes. The apps are designed to work 100% offline, so you can use them while travelling, in airplane mode, at retreats, while camping or whenever you simply want to disconnect.

Which app is best for meditation?

Meditation Bell & Zen Timer is the natural choice if you want a traditional meditation timer with Tibetan singing bowl bells, interval chimes and optional ambient sounds. Solfeggio Frequencies and Sound Bath Zen offer additional approaches based around frequency listening.

Which app is best for sleep?

All three apps can be used as part of a relaxing bedtime routine. Meditation Bell provides rain, waves, Brown Noise and other ambient sounds, while Solfeggio Frequencies includes a 4 Hz Delta option. Sound Bath Zen allows you to combine frequencies and calming sounds into your own personalised sleep environment.

Can I use the apps for studying and focus?

Yes. Meditation Bell includes Brown Noise and ambient soundscapes for focused sessions. Solfeggio Frequencies includes a 40 Hz Gamma option, while Sound Bath Zen lets you combine focus-oriented frequencies with sounds such as Brown Noise and atmospheric ambience.

Do I need an account?

No. The apps are designed to provide their core experiences without requiring you to create an account or social profile.
