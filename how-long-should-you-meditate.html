How Long Should You Meditate?

There is no single perfect meditation length. The best starting point is a session you can return to consistently — whether that means five minutes, ten minutes, twenty minutes or a longer practice.

A meditation timer is simply a practical tool for giving your practice a clear beginning and end. You can adjust the duration as your routine develops without needing to follow a fixed schedule.

The short answer

For many people, 5–10 minutes is a practical starting point. As meditation becomes part of your routine, you can gradually experiment with 15, 20 or 30-minute sessions. Longer sessions can also be useful when they fit comfortably into your routine. Consistency matters more than chasing a particular number of minutes.

The bigger picture

Consistency usually matters more than duration.

It can be tempting to think that a longer meditation automatically means a better meditation. In practice, a session that fits naturally into your routine can be much easier to maintain.

A short session you return to regularly can be a more practical foundation than an ambitious session that becomes difficult to maintain.

That is why a simple timer can be useful. Set the duration, begin your practice and let the timer take care of the ending.

A simple approach

Build your practice gradually.

  • Start with a manageable session.
  • Use the same time of day when possible.
  • Let the timer mark the beginning and end.
  • Increase the duration only when it feels natural.
  • Keep your practice simple enough to repeat.
Different moments need different sessions

Your ideal meditation length can change.

There is a difference between choosing a meditation length for a quick reset, a focused practice or a longer evening session. If you also use background audio, you can explore our guide to the best meditation sounds and choose an environment that suits your session.

For beginners

Keep the first sessions short and approachable. Five or ten minutes can be enough to learn how sitting with a timer feels without making the practice seem overwhelming.

For focus

A defined session can create a clear period in which you step away from distractions. Ten, twenty or thirty minutes may work depending on your routine.

For relaxation

You may prefer a slower, longer session when you have more time to unwind. Ambient sounds or gentle bell intervals can help create a calm structure.

Before sleep

A short evening meditation can provide a quiet transition into bedtime. A dark screen and gentle audio can be particularly useful for a late session.
