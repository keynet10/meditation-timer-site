Focus without the clutter

A simple timer can turn meditation into a manageable focus break.

For some people with ADHD, starting a meditation practice can feel harder than the meditation itself. Long sessions, complicated instructions and the awareness of time passing can all become distractions.

A simple meditation timer removes one of those decisions. Choose how long you want to practise, start the session and let the timer provide the structure.

The aim is not to make concentration perfect. It is to create a repeatable opportunity to pause, notice where your attention has gone and gently bring it back.

A practical routine

Try a short ADHD focus meditation session.

Keep the routine deliberately simple. The objective is to reduce the number of decisions between deciding to practise and actually beginning.

STEP 01

Choose your time

Start with a duration that feels realistic. Five or ten minutes can be enough for a short reset.

STEP 02

Set your sound

Choose silence, bells, brown noise or another soundscape depending on your environment.

STEP 03

Focus gently

Bring your attention to your breathing, body, surroundings or chosen sound.

STEP 04

Let the bell finish

Allow the timer to signal the end rather than repeatedly checking how much time remains.
