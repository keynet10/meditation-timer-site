A simpler approach

Meditation can be structured around your attention, rather than against it.

For people who find traditional meditation difficult, the hardest part can sometimes be the beginning. Deciding how long to sit, watching the clock and wondering when the session will finish can all become distractions.

A dedicated meditation timer removes much of that decision-making. Set the session, choose your sounds if you want them, and let the timer handle the ending.

Timer + mindfulness

A timer gives your attention somewhere to return to.

Meditation is not about never becoming distracted. When attention wanders, the practice is simply to notice it and return.

Keep the process simple.

Instead of worrying about whether you are meditating correctly, establish a small routine that you can repeat.

Choose your duration. Start the timer. Notice when your attention wanders. Return to the breath, body, sound or chosen object of attention.

  • Choose your own session length
  • Use interval bells if helpful
  • Listen to calming soundscapes
  • Keep the interface minimal
  • Use Dark Screen mode
  • Practice without a guide
