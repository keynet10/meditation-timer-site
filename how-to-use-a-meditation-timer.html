Step by step

How to use a meditation timer.

The process can be remarkably simple. The important part is creating a session you can actually begin.

STEP 01

Find a comfortable place

Sit somewhere where you can remain relatively undisturbed for the duration of your session.

STEP 02

Set the timer

Choose your desired duration before you begin. Start with a manageable amount of time.

STEP 03

Begin your practice

Close your eyes if comfortable and bring your attention to your chosen object of focus.

STEP 04

Follow the ending bell

When the timer finishes, allow the bell to mark the natural end of the session.

How long?

Choose a session that fits the moment.

The ideal meditation duration is personal. A shorter session that you actually complete can be more useful to your routine than a longer session you continually postpone.

5 min

Quick reset

Useful when you want a short pause during a busy day or need a simple starting point.

10 min

Everyday session

A manageable length for creating a regular meditation habit.

20 min

Deeper practice

A longer period for people who are comfortable remaining with their practice for more time.

30+ min

Extended session

Suitable when you have deliberately set aside more time for an uninterrupted practice.
