Frequently asked

A few things you might want to know

Is Meditation Bell Timer a guided meditation app?
No. It is designed primarily as an unguided meditation timer. You choose the duration, sounds and interval bells, then practise in your own way.
Can I use it without an internet connection?
Yes. The app is designed to work offline, making it suitable for meditation, travel, bedtime and situations where you don't want to rely on a connection.
Does the app contain advertising?
No. Meditation Bell Timer is designed around a distraction-free experience without advertising.
Can I use the timer for sleep and relaxation?
Yes. Longer sessions, calming ambient soundscapes and the pitch-black interface make it suitable for bedtime listening and relaxation routines.
Can I try the timer before committing to longer sessions?
Yes. The app includes a free short-session experience, allowing you to try the timer and soundscapes before deciding whether you want longer sessions.
