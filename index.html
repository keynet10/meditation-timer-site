The idea

A quieter approach to meditation.

Meditation doesn't need another complicated app competing for your attention. Meditation Bell Timer was created around a simple idea: give you a reliable timer, beautiful sounds and gentle bells — then get out of the way. Whether you're sitting for five minutes, settling down for the night, studying, practising mindfulness or simply taking a quiet break, the experience is designed to remain calm, focused and distraction-free.

Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

What is Meditation Bell Timer?

Meditation Bell Timer is a simple timer for meditation, mindfulness, focus, study, relaxation and sleep. It combines timed sessions with Tibetan bell sounds, interval chimes and immersive ambient soundscapes.

Can I use Meditation Bell Timer without an account?

Yes. The app is designed without requiring an account or social profile, keeping the experience simple and private.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Meditation Bell Timer is designed for offline use, making it useful when you want your meditation session to remain free from notifications and connectivity issues.

What sounds are included?

The sound library includes Tibetan bells, Tibetan singing bowls, rain, ocean waves, Brown Noise, rainforest ambience, birds, Om chant, campfire, steam train, evening cicadas and deep space ambience.

Can I set recurring meditation bells?

Yes. You can configure recurring bell intervals so a gentle Tibetan bell can mark points throughout your session.

Are there other apps in the collection?

Yes. The Meditation Bell Timer collection also includes Solfeggio Frequencies Healing and Sound Bath Zen. Each app has a different focus while sharing the same minimalist approach to digital wellness.
